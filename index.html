What Is Bombshell

Bombshell is a spy-themed twist on the "Shell Game" for 3-6 agents with an explosive ending.

It's quick to learn and family friendly, making it accessible for players of all skill levels!

High Stakes, Hidden Threats

Your mission is to find and take the Briefcase full of money...or will you end up with one of the bombs when time runs out?

The deck is your arsenal

11 unique Action Cards with skills and gadgets to aid you!

No player elimination

All but one agent will explode when the time runs out, ensuring everyone is in the game until the very end!
